## #sens-help (pin this)

```
# Sens help 🎯

Post your **game, sens and DPI**, and what feels off (overflicking, can't track, too slow to turn).

Quick tools:
• `/sens` converts between games, e.g. `/sens from:Valorant sens:0.35 to:CS2`
• The app's **Sens finder** mode tests 5 sens values and tells you which one you aim best with
• After any Tracking or Flick round, the **sens coach** says if you're overshooting or undershooting

Rule of thumb: most good players are between 25 and 50 cm per 360°.
```
