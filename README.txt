AIM TRAINER
===========

Practise tracking, flicking and reaction time for Minecraft, Roblox, CS2,
Valorant, Fortnite, Overwatch 2, Apex Legends or any game with CS2-style sens.

HOW TO START
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click AimTrainer.exe. Nothing needs installing.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
   "Run anyway". This happens because the app is new and not code-signed.
4. Enter a player name and your mouse DPI, then pick your game and type your
   in-game sensitivity.

CONTROLS
- Space: start a round / play again
- Esc: stop a round or go back

LEADERBOARD
1. Ask the owner to share the leaderboard page with you (as Editor).
2. Record your round (Xbox Game Bar: Win+Alt+R, or Medal).
3. On the results screen, click "Copy score code".
4. Open the leaderboard, go to "Submit a score", paste the code and add your
   clip. A mod will verify it.

Leaderboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/2qWk4AinKqbFxrjk6AHboB

Needs Windows 10 or 11. Your scores and settings are saved in
%APPDATA%\AimTrainer on your own PC.
