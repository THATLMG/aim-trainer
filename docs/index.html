Set your sens

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows that for apps that aren't code-signed, which costs hundreds of dollars a year. The app's full source code is public on GitHub. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

How do I find my mouse DPI?

Open your mouse software (Logitech G Hub, Razer Synapse, SteelSeries GG and so on). Most gaming mice ship at 800 or 1600 DPI. DPI only changes the cm/360 number shown; your aim already feels the same as in-game.

Does it work on Mac or phones?

Yes. Play in your browser on Mac, Chromebook or Linux, with all five modes, the sens coach and leaderboard submit. Use Chrome or Edge for exact sensitivity. A Mac app is on the way; the Windows app adds music, lyrics and Discord status.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Copy the score code from the results screen, make a free account on the leaderboard and submit it. You rank right away as Unverified. To get the ✓ Verified badge, record the round (Xbox Game Bar: Win+Alt+R, or Medal) and add the clip; a mod reviews it within 1–3 business days.
